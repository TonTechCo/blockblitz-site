What to include in a bug report

  • Your iPhone model + iOS version (Settings → General → About)
  • What you were doing when it happened (mode, theme, action)
  • Whether the issue reproduces every time, or only sometimes
  • A screenshot or short screen recording if relevant

Refund requests

In-app purchases are processed by Apple, not by us. Refunds go through Apple's standard request flow:

  • Open reportaproblem.apple.com
  • Sign in with the Apple ID that made the purchase
  • Pick the Block Blitzer transaction and submit the request

We don't have access to Apple's refund decision, but if Apple denies the refund and you feel it was a legitimate issue (e.g. a charge for a purchase you didn't make), email us and we'll do what we can.

Restore Purchases

If you switched devices or reinstalled the app and your IAPs aren't restored, tap Settings → Restore Purchases in the app. The Apple ID that made the original purchases must be signed in to the App Store on the device.
